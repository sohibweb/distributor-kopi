## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, tactile luxury visual identity tailored for high-volume enterprise coffee commerce, single-origin exporter operations, and commercial roastery procurement. The aesthetic combines traditional Indonesian agricultural prestige with rigorous modern commodity trading interfaces.

### Target Audience & Persona
- **Enterprise Green Coffee Buyers:** Roastery operators, commercial procurement directors, and importers requiring immediate visibility over moisture levels, lot sizes, cupping scores (SCA), harvest dates, and export certifications.
- **Estate & Cooperative Partners:** Producers seeking a transparent, prestigious platform reflecting craftsmanship and global export standards.

### Design Movement: Earthy Editorial Precision
A disciplined hybrid of **Tactile Luxury** and **Modern Corporate Trade**:
- Deep, roasted espresso tones dominate structural surfaces, creating an intimate, museum-grade container for raw agricultural data.
- Crisp parchment overlays and micro-fine hair-thin borders introduce a sense of certified export documentation, bill-of-lading precision, and physical cupping card authenticity.
- High-density tabular data balances expressive typographic scale, grounding visual majesty in cold transactional capability.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from dark roast depths, roasted coffee parchment, and physical cupping lab records. The primary canvas operates in a dark, atmospheric mode to evoke direct-from-origin warehouse exclusivity.

### Primary & Canvas Tiers
- **Primary Canvas (`#1F150C`):** Base canvas background representing unground dark roast. Deep, warm black-brown with zero cold grey undertones.
- **Canvas Absolute (`#000000`):** Strict structural contrast for fixed global app bars, data sheet tables, modal underlays, and footer anchor bars.
- **Container Secondary (`#412D15`):** Mid-tone coffee wood and secondary surface fill. Used for panel cards, active interactive states, border strokes, and segmented controls.

### Accent & Neutral Hierarchy
- **Parchment Neutral (`#E1DCC9`):** Represents raw coffee parchment (pergamino) and vintage cupping sheets. Primary text color on dark surfaces; also serves as the high-contrast background fill for physical sample cards, wholesale invoice tags, and primary CTAs.
- **Amber Gold Accent (`#C2934D`):** Reserved for high-value metrics: SCA cupping scores (85+ Specialty Grade), export qualification badges, and certification insignias (Fair Trade, Organic, Rainforest Alliance).
- **Crisp Neutral White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-impact contrast for headline typography, real-time metric indicators, and primary data values.

### State & Utility Roles
- **Border Subtle:** `rgba(225, 220, 201, 0.12)` — hairline demarcation separating harvest lots.
- **Border Strong:** `rgba(225, 220, 201, 0.28)` — active focus rings and selection states.
- **Status Success (In Stock / Port Ready):** `#6E8B3D` (Subdued Olive).
- **Status Warning (Low Lot Stock):** `#D9822B` (Burnt Orange).

## Typography

The typographical pairing splits purpose strictly between brand authority and trade execution:

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides architectural presence for headlines, display metrics, and hero origin titles. Its geometric construction carries modern Indonesian trade authority without feeling overly sterile or decorative.
- **Inter** carries all dense procurement data, cupping profiles (Aroma, Acidity, Body, Finish), moisture metrics, weight volumes, and pricing tables. It ensures total legibility down to dense 11px table footers.
- Use `label-mono` with uppercase transformation for lot identifiers (e.g., `LOT-ACEH-GAYO-2024-08`), container tracking, screen size grades (Grade 1, TP, Large Bean), and incoterms (FOB Belawan / CIF Hamburg).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model prioritizes strict enterprise alignment and predictable reading paths for commercial transactions:

- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max Width 1440px):** 12-column layout with fixed side canvas padding. 24px gutters create crisp separations between lot catalogs, origin maps, cupping radar charts, and volume quotation calculators.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1023px):** Collapses dual-panel origin inspection into vertically stacked sections; specifications table remains side-by-side with bulk order configuration.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px):** Single-column stack. Edge padding locks to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), ensuring technical data tables scroll horizontally with locked freeze-columns for Coffee Variety and Score.
- **Rhythm Principle:** Internal card padding is strictly uniform using `space-md` (`1rem`) for standard specification blocks and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for high-level deal terms and order summary drawers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic floating drop shadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **low-contrast parchment outlines** characteristic of high-end physical trade ledgers:

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** `#1F150C` flat surface for whole-page viewport grounding.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Section Containers):** `#000000` or `#281B0F` with hairline stroke `1px solid rgba(225, 220, 201, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Sample Drawers):** `#2A1E12` with a structured stroke `1px solid rgba(225, 220, 201, 0.22)` coupled with an ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modal / Document Viewers):** Deep black `#0A0704` surface with high-contrast amber rim lighting: `0 0 0 1px #412D15, 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.
- **Inverted Elevation (Parchment Manifests):** When rendering official certificates of origin or cupping manifests, the surface switches to `#E1DCC9` flat paper with `#1F150C` high-contrast typography, completely devoid of shadows to resemble physical paper stock.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a disciplined **Soft (`1`)** shape language:
- Standard buttons, form fields, filter chips, and card corners utilize a restrained `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Large modals, wholesale summary sheets, and map cards utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- This near-orthogonal geometry honors industrial coffee packaging, export shipping crates, standard burlap sacks (karung goni), and structured commodity exchange tables. Circular pills are strictly forbidden except for live status dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Trade CTA:** `#E1DCC9` (Parchment Cream) background with `#1F150C` text, bold weight (`label-lg`), 4px radius. On hover, subtle transition to `#FFFFFF` with slight outward glow `0 0 12px rgba(225, 220, 201, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Wholesale Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(225, 220, 201, 0.3)` border, `#E1DCC9` text. Hover transitions to `#412D15` background and `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Understated `#E1DCC9` text with 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline; hover transitions to `#C2934D` (Amber Gold).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Container:** Dark `#140E08` fill with `1px solid #412D15` border.
- **Typography:** `#FFFFFF` input text; `#E1DCC9` (50% opacity) placeholder text.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#C2934D` (Amber) with zero fuzzy outer rings; razor-sharp 1px border highlight.
- **Steppers (Bulk Metric Tonnage / Bag Count):** Integrated segmented counter embedded inside the field using `#000000` buttons flanking the numeric display.

### Specification & Inventory Cards
- **Dark Lot Card:** Background `#23180E`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(225, 220, 201, 0.1)`. Features a pinned micro-header showing Origin Province (e.g., *Sumatra Gayo*, *Toraja Sapan*, *Bali Kintamani*).
- **Inverted Spec Manifest Card:** Background `#E1DCC9`, typography `#1F150C`. Used exclusively for laboratory cupping reports, lot certifications, and bill-of-lading confirmation receipts.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Cupping Score Badge (SCA 80+):** Bordered badge with `#412D15` background, `#C2934D` text, and a hairline `1px solid #C2934D` stroke.
- **Processing Chip:** Pill-less rectangular tag (`0.25rem` radius), uppercase `label-mono`, `#000000` background, `#E1DCC9` border and text (e.g., *WET HULLED / GILING BASAH*, *FULL WASHED*, *ANAEROBIC NATURAL*).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16px geometry with 2px radius.
- Unchecked: `#140E08` base with `1px solid #412D15`.
- Checked: `#C2934D` background with `#1F150C` checkmark indicator.

### Specialized B2B Domain Components
- **Cupping Score Attribute Bar:** Compact horizontal gauge displaying fragrance, aroma, flavor, aftertaste, and acidity from 6.00 to 10.00 scale, rendered using `#C2934D` filled segments against a `#412D15` track.
- **Moisture & Density Metric Tile:** Monospaced data readout displaying water activity (`aW`), moisture content (`11.2%`), and bean density (`680 g/L`) with micro-labels in uppercase `label-mono`.
- **Harvest & Batch Tracker:** Fixed bottom status bar displaying real-time sea cargo container allocation and remaining 60kg burlap bag counts per lot.
